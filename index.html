REVIEW · SCREEN 5 OF 6

The anatomy of a great ping

Remember this structure every time.

1

Emotion

Acknowledge what they're feeling. Make it personal.

2

Insight / Intrigue

Hint at something valuable from the session they haven't fully explored.

3

Invitation to return

Give them a clear, warm reason to come back.

EXAMPLE PING

"Hi dear, I can feel how heavy and confusing this situation is for you after everything you shared with me. I keep thinking about the 'when' we touched during our conversation — there's a specific detail that can change the way you act next (and it's important you don't miss it). If you're ready, come back and I'll explain what to do step-by-step."

Key reminders

  • • Always use the Pings tab in NebulaX
  • • One ping = one complete message (never split)
  • • Send within 24 hours after a chat
  • • Personalize — reference something from the session
