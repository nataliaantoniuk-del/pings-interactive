REVIEW · SCREEN 7 OF 8

Your Mandatory tab rules

Keep these close. Every shift, every time.

1

Start every shift with Mandatory

Open NebulaX → Pings tab → Mandatory. Work through the list before anything else. These are your highest-priority clients.

2

Always use the Pings tab

Sending through regular chat doesn't count. Only pings sent through the Pings tab appear in your analytics. No exceptions.

3

One ping = one complete message

Never split a ping into multiple messages. One personal message with the full structure — emotion, intrigue, invitation.

4

Cover 85%+ of your Mandatory list

Distribute throughout your shift: start → middle → end. Don't leave clients uncontacted. That's where the metric drops.

EXAMPLE PING

"Hi dear, I can feel how heavy and confusing this situation is for you after everything you shared with me. I keep thinking about what we touched on — there's a specific detail that can change the way you act next (and it's important you don't miss it). If you're ready, come back and I'll explain what to do step-by-step."
